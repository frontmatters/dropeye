How it works

No accounts. No servers. No cloud.

Dropeye runs a tiny board server right on your Mac. Anything on the same network joins in seconds.

01

Run Dropeye

It lives in your menu bar and starts a local board server in-process. No account, no setup, no daemon.

02

Open the board

Scan the QR or visit the address on any phone, tablet or Mac on the same Wi-Fi. Bonjour finds it too.

03

Share instantly

Drop text, code or files on the board and they appear on every device, live — over plain HTTP/SSE.

Features

Small app. Sharp edges.

Everything you need to move things between devices — and nothing that phones home.

Share your clipboard — ⌘⇧V

One hotkey pushes your Mac clipboard onto the board; copy any item back with a click. Manual only — Dropeye never auto-captures.

Code-aware

Snippets are detected and rendered in Fira Code, indentation intact.

Search the board

A filter field hides non-matching items as you type — live, even as new ones arrive.

Encrypted at rest

The live session and shared files are AES-256-GCM encrypted with a per-Mac Keychain key.

Scan to join

QR codes and Bonjour discovery get devices onto the board with zero typing. Both optional.

Vaults, in & out

Export and re-import encrypted .dropeye-vault bundles — a full round-trip.

Notifications that respect you

Know the moment someone shares something — the alert carries only who and what kind, never the content. Bursts coalesce into one.

Privacy & security

Nothing leaves your network. No servers to trust, because there aren't any.

  • Passphrase gate + TailscaleOn trusted Wi-Fi it's plain local HTTP. For anything untrusted: a passphrase gate and an encrypted Tailscale tunnel.
  • Encrypted at restAES-256-GCM for the session and files; named vaults are passphrase-protected.
  • Sandboxed & App-Store-readyNo JIT, no bundled runtime, no unsigned code. A single native Swift binary.
